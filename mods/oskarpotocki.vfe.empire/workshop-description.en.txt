Vanilla Factions Expanded - Empire — Russian supplement (12 strings)

Completes Mewn Goddess's main Russian pack: ten messages for noble arrival/waiting, ascent-parade cancellation and ending, plus the court stimulant's label and description. In both actual test launches these 12 fields were English without the supplement and Russian with it. Existing translations are preserved; no third-party Russian text is copied. The VFE - Empire brand title remains English.

Mewn Goddess's main Russian pack:

Royalty DLC, Harmony and Vanilla Expanded Framework, as required by the original.

Load this supplement after the original and main Russian pack. Supported RimWorld version: 1.7. It contains 12 additional strings; the main Russian pack is mandatory.

Testing: actual isolated English-control and Russian-candidate launches on RimWorld 1.7.4990 rev1069. Royalty and the main Russian pack were active in both. All 12 English, then Russian game values were verified: ten owned Keyed entries and two physical effect-definition fields with correct sources. The full localization error multiset stayed unchanged: 71 → 71; zero new errors. This is startup/field-readback testing. The parade itself, colony completion, every formatted-name scenario and subscription installation of the published Workshop copy were not tested by these runs.
