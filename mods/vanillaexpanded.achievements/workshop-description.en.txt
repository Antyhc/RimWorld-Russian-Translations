Vanilla Achievements Expanded — Russian completion

One descriptive Odyssey achievement title: “Laying Down Foundations” → “Закладывая основу”. This title remained English in the existing Russian pack. Preserved game names, the quotation and the DLC acronym are left intact; no third-party Russian text is copied.

Installation: requires the original mod and its dependencies. Load this supplement after the original. Its string loads only while Odyssey DLC is active; without Odyssey this pack injects no translation. Supported RimWorld version: 1.7.

Testing: actual RimWorld 1.7.4990 rev1069 startup in isolated profiles. With Odyssey, the English control and Russian candidate title were confirmed by reading the game field. Without Odyssey, the definition remained absent and no translation was injected. The same exact payload was used in all four launches. The full localization error multiset was unchanged within each pair: 38 → 38 without Odyssey, 98 → 98 with Odyssey; zero new errors. This supplement does not claim to fix those baseline errors. Extended gameplay and installation of the published Workshop copy through subscription were not tested by these runs.
