Supplement to Customize Weapon's built-in Russian localization for RimWorld 1.7. Adds 23 missing ability, damage, job, work-giver and charge-name fields. Requires the original Customize Weapon by Vortex and Harmony. Load after the original. AI-assisted translation and editing with OpenAI Codex.

https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3550585103

Separate original/candidate startups on RimWorld 1.7.5000 rev1275 verified all 23 physical values, including four nested charge nouns. Localization errors and logs matched the control run, with no additional errors. All 207 built-in Russian keys were preserved. Weapon dialog layout, colony gameplay and independent human proofreading were not tested.
